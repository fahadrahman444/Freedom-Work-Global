3. 1. Learn the four fundamentals of typography
Font family

The design of the letters. For B2B agency websites, clean sans-serif fonts work well.

    Inter — modern and highly readable.

    Manrope — clean with a slightly distinctive personality.

    Roboto — familiar and functional.

Font weight

Controls how thick or prominent the text appears.

Regular 400 — body text

Medium 500 — labels and navigation

Semibold 600 — section headings

Bold 700 — main headlines
Font size

Establishes importance. Your main headline should be much larger than your body text.

Example: H1 at 56 px, H2 at 36 px, body at 18 px.
Line height

Controls the vertical distance between lines of text.

Example: Body text at 18 px with a 28 px line height is comfortable to read.

3. Keep paragraphs readable and headings distinct
Good
Build a Stronger Sales Pipeline

We help B2B companies reach qualified prospects through focused research and personalized outreach.

Short paragraphs · Clear hierarchy
Avoid

BUILD A STRONGER SALES PIPELINE WE HELP B2B COMPANIES REACH QUALIFIED PROSPECTS THROUGH FOCUSED RESEARCH AND PERSONALIZED OUTREACH.

All caps · Weak hierarchy · Dense text

Follow these rules:

    Keep paragraph line lengths around 45–80 characters when practical.

    Use sentence case for most headings and paragraphs.

    Use bold text sparingly to emphasize important information.

    Give headings enough space above and below them.

    Avoid using more than two font families unless you have a strong design reason.
